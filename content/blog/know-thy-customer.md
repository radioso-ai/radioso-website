**Your web page used to be like a store front.** Until now, you may have let customers browse the aisles, observed what they were looking at, and inferred what they wanted from their behavior. You could occasionally ask for feedback.

**Now people ask your AI assistant.** In our experience about **0.5-2% of visitors** do, depending on the organization and how prominently the assistant is displayed. At 50k monthly visitors that can mean **500 conversations a month** where people tell you why they came.

On one site, people prefer asking the assistant for course dates rather than browsing course pages. Turned out most people already knew what course they wanted to take, **they just needed the details.**

> Our favourite example: nobody clicks a product you don't sell.

At a web store we work with, several customers asked about a specific perfumery category. The store didn't sell perfumes. **Guess what, they do now.**
